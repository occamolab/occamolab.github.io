LawKAN

Calibration without the accuracy floor.

Real robot arms deviate from nominal kinematics: joint compliance, play, and tolerances deform the composition law itself. Classical calibration assumes the nominal law and fits per-joint offsets, so it cannot represent such a deformation — accuracy stalls at a floor that more poses will not remove.

LawKAN puts the exact composition law into the layer and learns only its deformation. On real arms from ABB, KUKA, Franka and Kinova, a correction of a few hundred parameters beats classical Gauss–Newton calibration — small enough to audit, cheap enough to run at the edge.
