Advantages

  • Lower error where deformation matters. Where hardware deforms the kinematic law, LawKAN removes the accuracy floor left by the classical estimator. Where there is no deformation, it matches the classical fit and does not degrade it.
  • Diagnostic by design. The small learned correction is inspectable and shows where and how the hardware deviates from the nominal law — not just a black-box accuracy gain.
  • Smaller and stronger at small N. 306 learned values vs tens of thousands for a tuned MLP; lower error on small pose sets, small enough for the edge.

LawKAN

How it works

LawKAN keeps the kinematic law fixed and learns only its deviation. A small correction absorbs compliance, play, and tolerance effects; a pre-training span check says whether the data can support it.

Limits

Not every case is a win. Where the classical fit already sits at the noise floor (BROAD IMU, gyroscope calibration), the law layer buys a smaller model, not lower error. On UR5 and Kinova sockets the classical fit is at least as good. Parity is still useful, but we do not call it a win.
