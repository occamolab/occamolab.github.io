LawKAN

Calibration without the accuracy floor.

Industrial robot arms deviate from nominal kinematics: joint compliance, play, and tolerances deform the composition law itself. Classical per-joint calibration cannot represent such a deformation, so accuracy stalls at a floor that more poses will not remove.

LawKAN puts the exact composition law into the layer and learns only its deformation. On arms from ABB, KUKA, Franka and Kinova, a few-hundred-parameter correction beats classical Gauss–Newton — small enough to audit, cheap enough to run at the edge.

LawKAN: Group-Law Layers for Composition — When Imposing a Known Law Pays Off
