Privacy

OccamoLab, independent research. Contact: hello@occamolab.org.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive the fields you submit: name, email, organisation, the topic you selected, your task description, an optional link or data description, and your consent.

Why we use it

To reply to your request, assess whether the law-layer approach fits your task, and discuss a possible pilot or integration. We do not sell your data.

Processors

The form is processed by Formspree and delivered to our email inbox. The site uses GoatCounter for aggregate, cookie-less page and click statistics; it does not build a personal profile.

Retention

We keep contact requests for up to 12 months after the last exchange, unless a pilot or contract requires a different retention period. You can ask us to delete your data earlier.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, or object to processing by emailing hello@occamolab.org. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Consent

By submitting the form you agree to be contacted about your request. You can withdraw consent at any time by email.
